Safety & Trail Etiquette

Small habits that keep you and the mountains safe.

Start early
Begin by 5–6 AM to avoid heat and finish before dark.
Hydrate
Carry 2–3 litres and sip every 20 minutes.
Leave no trace
Carry back all plastic and wrappers. Don't pluck plants.
Tell someone
Share your route and return time with family.
Check permits
Many forest treks need Forest Dept. permission or a guide.
Monsoon caution
Leeches, slippery rocks, swollen streams — avoid risky crossings.
